Pregnant Woman Pees Herself in Front of Me

Author: MomoCat

*000*2022-12-25 12:41

Hi everyone!

Here’s an incident I witnessed at work a few years ago.

I used to work at a grocery store. I was in all kinds of departments, but this particular day I was working self checkout. For those who don’t know, I basically have to monitor and help those who use the self-checkout machines. There were six (I think?) at the time I was working there, so it was pretty busy. This happened around 11PM which is important because we lock the customer bathrooms at 10PM to reduce theft or something.

So it was pretty busy that night (it’s always busy) and I was just doing my job watching the machines, when suddenly this woman approached me asking why the bathrooms were locked. She was very pretty— maybe early-mid thirties, Asian, with long hair pulled back into a ponytail. She was wearing a long dress that went down to her ankles, and most prominently, she was very pregnant. I’m talking 7 months at the very least.

”We lock the bathrooms at 10, it’s to prevent theft or something,” I said.

”Really? Oh, no. I have to go so bad!” She whined. Like, literally whined. My face turned bright red.

”I can see if there’s someone in the store yet who can unlock it for you,” I offered. Our manager usually has the keys and he was supposed to be there until closing. “I’m sure he would be willing to.”

”Yes, please do that.” She said with a nervous nod. She was shifting from one foot to the other.

I paged the manager to come to the front of the store, but for one reason or another, he couldn’t come right away. I went about my job with the woman waiting nearby. A few seconds passed. Maybe a minute.

”Will he be here soon?” She asked. She wasn’t holding herself, but I could tell she wanted to. I’ve never seen anyone so desperate right in front of me.

”Should be,” I said. “If he’s not here in like thirty seconds, I’ll page him again.”

”Thank you!” She said with a smile. I felt terrible for her, she was deeply embarrassed being so desperate in front of me—but also feeling like an inconvenience. I assured her I understood.

Fortunately, my manager came shortly after that. Before I could even say anything, she jumped in.

”Can you please unlock the bathrooms? I have to go so bad!” She said, and gestured to her pregnant belly, “I’m pregnant and I literally can’t hold it.”

He said something about how we usually don’t do this for customers but he understood her situation. Then he handed me the keys and I walked with her to the bathroom.

I swear, I didn’t do this on purpose. I know that I could promise up and down that this was an accident and you guys wouldn’t believe me, but trust me. This was entirely unintentional.

I could not get the door open.

I’ve never actually had to unlock the bathrooms before, and the lock was weird. Like not the usual put-the-key-in-and-twist lock. I struggled with it for a few seconds while she was almost literally dancing with how badly she needed to go.

”Is there something wrong?” She asked, peeking at the lock over my shoulder.

”I’ve never had to unlock the door before, so— oh!” I said as it suddenly clicked open.

I held the door open for her as she ran inside. As she rushed in, I could see little dribbles of pee at her feet. She was leaking. There was a small but still very present trail of urine behind her as she rushed into a stall. She was wearing a dress, so I imagine all the pee just dripped out with nothing in the way. I was in shock, I’ve never seen anything like this before! I really wanted to hear how satisfying her stream was, but even though we’re both women, I knew it was best to give her some privacy. I closed the door and stood outside to make sure no one went in so I could lock it immediately after she was done.

She left the bathroom once she was done and the look on her face told me she was extremely embarrassed.

”I’m sorry, you might want to send someone to clean the floor in there,” she said. I swear my eyes were practically bulging out of my head.

”I’ll send someone in,” I said. “I totally understand, sometimes it happens.”

Her expression softened and she went on her way.

I locked the bathroom door and returned to my job. But I couldn’t stop thinking about this for the rest of the day.
