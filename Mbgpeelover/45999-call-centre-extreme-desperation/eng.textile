Call centre fun that I got to witness

For a while as a student I had an evening job in a call centre. It was one of those tax dodging places that one night I turned up to and it had closed down and no-one ever heard of them ever again. This one was for fitted kitchens and we had this really dodgy sales pitch to call people about with something about ‘if you had to get a free kitchen how many cupboards would you like?’ Of course we all knew it was a con but hey I was a student and needed cash!

So the sales folk were literally in the room next door and we were targeted on every house we could get them to get into. It was high pressured and shifts were three hours long with no break. It was mind numbing boring and repetitive but it was also easy!

We all used to bring water or other drinks with us as the continual talking would make your voice dry so by the end of the three hours I got to see a few people (male and female) more than a little uncomfortable with full bladders. That was always my favourite bit of course!

There were four rows of phones with four of us to a table and a table at the side for supervisors. Every time someone showed a slight interest in the call we would pass to the supervisor who would attempt to book a home visit.

This one night was going fairly ok and a few salespeople had actually got to leave the showroom. There was a buoyancy about the room and spirits were high. I noticed one supervisor, let’s call her Suzanne, was drinking cola by the bottle and by this point had drank two individual bottles and had just opened a can! I was glancing at her as I dialled and made calls and sure enough about an hour into the shift she was bouncing her leg.

I should say at this point that we were quite literally in a portacabin and there were no toilets on the premises at all. It was a pop up, illegal, cash in hand job but we were all desperate for cash and Suzanne also seemed a little desperate for more than just cash that night!

About half an hour or so later, by now about half way through the shift, she was wriggling around a fair bit. Unlike the canvassers the supervisors had periods where they were not on calls and she would sip more cola or walk about listening to our calls. She seemed to need to walk about more and over time her movements were becoming more of a fidget than wandering, more a dance than checking on calls. I had a ‘bite’ and raised my hand to show the call was being passed over to Suzanne. She sat down and took the call going through the script with her legs tightly crossed and sitting forward on her seat.

To my amazement she continued to finish her can of cola despite clearly having a very full bladder.

The second half of the shift for me was all about Suzanne. When on calls she would swing her legs about in and out like scissors both forwards and backwards and side to side as she tried to stay focussed on her script. It was a good night and we were about to hit a big target which may have given us all a cash bonus so she was under pressure to perform as well as being under pressure from her very full bladder.

When not on the phone she would dance around the room in an incredible way and at one point she placed one leg over the end of the table I was sitting at and pushed the corner between her legs. What was amazing was that even though it was so obvious she was bursting for a pee she never once said anything! That was until the last half hour.

About 8:30 (our shift finished at 9) I heard Suzanne finally admit what everyone in the entire building had known for an hour. She had just finished another call where she has been seated with one hand on the phone and the other between her legs when she put the phone down, stood up and put both hands palm down on the table. She began marching quickly and said to no-one in particular ‘oh my God, I am so bursting for the bathroom right now’ which was hardly news to anyone!

After that there really was no holding her back. She openly held herself, rocked on her seat when not on calls and even went over to a corner where she bend down on her knees and bounced like she was about to make a huge puddle.

She lost all embarrassment and all her concentration was on holding back the flood between her legs. What amazed me was that the room was a mix of males and females and she had no care about that one bit. She stuck the corner of tables between her legs, had a hand between herself almost at all times and would go on and on about stuff like ‘oh my God I have never needed to pee this much in my life!’ ‘Ohhhhhhh this is so hard! I am so bursting and there is no toilet’ and so on and on.

She marched on the spot, bent over, rocked on a chair, sat crossed legged bolt upright and at times she was almost crying.

While I was clearly enjoying it it seemed not everyone was as one woman ended up getting angry and told her just to shut up and piss herself and stop acting like a child. I was so annoyed at that but clearly Suzanne had held as much as she could and she ran out the building with both hands between her legs crying.

At the end of the shift she was outside smoking having apparently ran around the cabin and peed outside.

I heard her say to one of her friends that she pissed that long her knees were sore from bending down.

I only wish I had got to see her pee that much because I can only imagine how much urine flowed out her that night.
